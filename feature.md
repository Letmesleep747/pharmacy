1. Medicine master data management
    Add, edit, and delete medicines.
    Store medicine name, generic name, strength, dosage form, category, manufacturer, barcode, and storage condition.

2. Category management
    Create and manage medicine categories (e.g., antibiotics, analgesics, cardiovascular, vitamins).

3. Supplier management
    Record supplier details (name, contact, address, email).
    Link suppliers to purchase orders and medicines.

4. Batch-level inventory tracking
    Record each medicine batch separately.
    Store batch number, quantity, expiry date, purchase price, selling price, supplier, and location.
    Track stock-in and stock-out per batch.

5. Stock-in (purchase receiving)
    Record new stock received from suppliers.
    Assign batch number and expiry date at receiving.
    Update inventory automatically.

6. Stock-out (dispensing/sales)
    Deduct stock when medicines are dispensed or sold.
    Select batch automatically using FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out).
    Record the batch actually dispensed.

7. Expiry tracking
    Identify expired, near-expiry, and safe stock.
    Show expiry status per medicine and per batch.
    Exclude expired or quarantined stock from normal dispensing.

8. Expiry alerts
    Alert staff when medicines are approaching expiry (e.g., 90, 60, 30 days).
    Highlight high-risk medicines on the dashboard.

9. FEFO recommendation
    Automatically recommend the batch with the earliest expiry date for dispensing.
    Enforce FEFO logic in stock selection.

10. Low-stock alerts
    Alert when stock falls below the minimum level.
    Show which medicines need attention.

11. Reorder recommendations
    Suggest when to reorder based on current stock, minimum level, and usage pattern.
    Help prevent both stockouts and overstocking.

12. Dashboard
    Show total medicines, total batches, expired stock count, near-expiry stock count, low-stock count.
    Provide quick overview of inventory health.

13. Reports
    Inventory report (by medicine, category, supplier).
    Expiry report (expired, near-expiry, safe).
    Stock movement report (stock-in, stock-out).
    Low-stock and reorder report.
    Supplier performance report.

14. User management and login
    Secure login for pharmacy staff.
    Different roles (e.g., admin, pharmacist, storekeeper, cashier).
    Access control for sensitive functions (e.g., stock adjustments, supplier management).

15. Audit trail / activity log
    Record who added, edited, or deleted medicines, batches, suppliers, and stock.
    Track stock adjustments and reasons.

16. Export to PDF/Excel
    Export reports for printing or further analysis.

17. Usable-stock calculation
    Distinguish between total stock and usable (non-expired, non-quarantined) stock.
    Show at-risk stock separately.

18. Quarantine / recall management
    Mark batches as quarantined, damaged, or recalled.
    Exclude them from normal dispensing.

## Core modules: Medicine, Category, Supplier, Batch Inventory, Stock-in/out, Expiry & Alerts, FEFO, Reorder, Dashboard, Reports, Users & Login, Audit Trail.
